# Weekly Fitness Article Plan — Kanhustle Fitness

Curated research and topic list for a weekly article series on kanhustlefitness.com.
Compiled September 2026. Angled toward Kanhustle's core audience — athletes,
cricketers, and strength/conditioning clients — with general evidence-based
fitness trends mixed in for broader reach.

---

## 1. Verified Sources to Pull From Every Week

Use these as your standing "source list" whenever researching or fact-checking
an article. All are peer-reviewed, institutional, or editorially rigorous —
safe to cite directly.

### Research bodies & journals
- [ACSM (American College of Sports Medicine)](https://acsm.org/) — the leading sports-medicine authority; publishes the annual Worldwide Fitness Trends survey
- [ACSM's Health & Fitness Journal](https://journals.lww.com/acsm-healthfitness/pages/default.aspx)
- [NASM (National Academy of Sports Medicine)](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center) — new peer-reviewed *Journal of Fitness, Wellness and Human Performance*
- [PubMed / NCBI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) — free access to sports-science and exercise-physiology studies
- [Examine.com](https://examine.com/) — independent, evidence-graded reviews of nutrition & supplement research (run by scientists/editors, no supplement sales)
- [ScienceDaily – Fitness & Sports Medicine](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/health_medicine/fitness/) — plain-language summaries of new peer-reviewed studies, good for weekly "new research" hooks

### Medical/health institutions
- [Mayo Clinic – Fitness](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness)
- [Harvard Health Publishing](https://www.health.harvard.edu/topics/exercise-and-fitness)
- [Cleveland Clinic – Exercise & Fitness](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/category/fitness)
- [Johns Hopkins Medicine – Health & Wellness](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/wellness-and-prevention)

### Sport-specific / performance authorities
- [NSCA (National Strength and Conditioning Association)](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/) — best source for strength & conditioning programming backed by research
- [BJSM (British Journal of Sports Medicine)](https://bjsm.bmj.com/) — top-tier sports injury/performance journal
- [ICC / BCCI sports science & cricket conditioning resources] — for cricket-specific bowling/fitness content, cross-reference NSCA and BJSM studies on fast-bowling workload and injury prevention

### Trend-tracking (for "what's hot" pieces)
- [ACSM Worldwide Fitness Trends survey (published annually)](https://acsm.org/top-fitness-trends-2026/)
- [NASM Trends Blog](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/top-fitness-trends)
- [ACE Fitness – Pro Resources](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/)

**Rule of thumb:** every article should cite at least one primary research source (PubMed/ACSM/NSCA/BJSM) plus one plain-language explainer (Mayo/Harvard/Examine) so content is both credible and readable.

## 3. Suggested Weekly Workflow

1. **Monday:** Pick next topic from the calendar above (or scan ScienceDaily's fitness section + ACSM/NSCA for anything newly published that week that beats the plan).
2. **Tuesday–Wednesday:** Draft article — lead with the research finding, translate into a practical takeaway for athletes/gym-goers, close with a Kanhustle program tie-in (camp, coaching, or bowling clinic).
3. **Thursday:** Fact-check every claim against a primary source (table above) and link it.
4. **Friday:** Publish to kanhustlefitness.com; share to Facebook/Instagram/YouTube.
5. **Ongoing:** Re-scan ACSM/NASM/NSCA monthly for new trend reports to refresh the queue past week 12.

## 4. Notes on Automated Scraping

A live, automated scraper for "trending" content wasn't run here — this list was
compiled via targeted research queries against the sources above (Sept 2026),
not a continuous crawler. For an ongoing pipeline, the practical options are:
- A scheduled research pass (weekly) repeating the queries in section 1, or
- An RSS/API integration with ACSM, NSCA, PubMed, and ScienceDaily's fitness feed,
  which would need to be built as a script (can be added to this repo on request).

No content was scraped from paywalled or non-authoritative sites; every link
above is a legitimate public source.
